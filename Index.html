Skylab
General Overview
Skylab was a space station which operated in LEO and had 4 total missions. Skylab-1 was the mission to launch the mission and Skylab-2 through 4 were crewed missions to perform science and any other work that needed to be done on Skylab. Skylab sat at a orbit of 434km by 441.9km and spent 6.1 years in orbit. Skylab had a pressurized volume of 351.6 cubic meters and was occupied by 9 people total. It was operated by NASA as the US' first ever space station after the apollo missions. Skylab crew were delivered via the apollo CSM which carried 3 crew to and from the space station.

Structure
Skylab was made out of 4 modules called: Apollo Telescope Mount(ATM), Multiple Docking Adaptor(MDA), Airlock Module(AM), and the Orbital Workshop(OW). Skylab was 11.1 meters tall, 17 meters wide and 25.1 meters long with a pressurized volume of 351.6 cubic meters. The Skylab was mostly made out of aluminium.

Launch
Skylab was launched on the 14th of May 1973 at 17:30:00 UTC on a Saturn V rocket out of Kennedy Space Center Launch Complex 39A. The launch of Skylab could be reffered to as Skylab 1. During the launch aerodynamic pressure forces caused a micromeateorite shield, sunshade and solar panel to tear off the station causing another solar panel to get stuck due to debris that got tangled in it. After the launch KSC LC-39A was deactivated to prepare it for the STS/Space Shuttle programme. During the launch of Skylab the Skylab 2 crew were on LC-39B for a launch 90 minutes later tho due to complications wsith Skylab the mission was aborted for a launch 2 weeks later for additional training due to the problems and to allow NASA to figure out what went wrong with the station. On the 25th of May Skylab 2 launched 11 days after Skylab 1.

EVA
Voskhod 2 performed 1 EVA lasting 12 minutes and 9 seconds conducted by Alexei Leonov. During the EVA Leonov's Berkut spacesuit started inflating due to the atmopshere of the spacesuit pushing against the vacuum of space so Leonov had to vent the suits atmosphere into space to allow his suit to deflate. If he was unable to vent the suit he couldnt have had reentered the airlock. The EVA started 18th of March 1965 at 08:34:51 UTC and ended 08:47:00 UTC on the same day.
