Skylab
General Overview
Skylab was a space station in LEO and had 3 crewed missions (Skylab 2–4). It orbited Earth at ~434–442 km and was NASA’s first space station.

Structure
Skylab had four main parts: ATM, MDA, AM, and Orbital Workshop. It was mostly aluminium and about 25 meters long.

Launch
Launched 14 May 1973 on Saturn V from LC-39A. It suffered solar panel damage during launch.
