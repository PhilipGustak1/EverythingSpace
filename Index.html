Skylab
General Overview
Skylab was a space station which operated in LEO and had 4 total missions. Skylab-1 was the launch mission and Skylab-2 through 4 were crewed missions. It orbited at ~434 km by 441.9 km and spent 6.1 years in space. It had a pressurized volume of 351.6 cubic meters and was occupied by 9 people total. It was NASA’s first space station after the Apollo program.

Structure
Skylab consisted of the Apollo Telescope Mount (ATM), Multiple Docking Adaptor (MDA), Airlock Module (AM), and Orbital Workshop (OW). It was primarily aluminium. Dimensions: 11.1 m tall, 17 m wide, 25.1 m long.

Launch
Skylab launched on 14 May 1973 at 17:30 UTC on a Saturn V from Kennedy Space Center LC-39A. The micrometeoroid shield and one solar panel were damaged during launch. Skylab 2 launched 11 days later after troubleshooting and training.
